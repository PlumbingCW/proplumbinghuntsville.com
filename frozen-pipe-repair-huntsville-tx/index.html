Frozen Pipe Repair in Huntsville, TX: What To Do First

If water has stopped at one faucet on a cold morning, a pipe is probably frozen. Open the faucet, shut off the main if you see any crack or bulge, and warm the pipe slowly. Never use a torch or open flame.

East Texas does not freeze often, which is exactly why homes here are rarely built for it. Pipes run through attics, exterior walls and open crawl spaces, so one hard night can catch a house by surprise.

Written by the Huntsville Pro Plumbing team · Published November 13, 2026.

Signs a pipe has frozen

The clearest sign is a faucet that gives a trickle or nothing at all while others in the house still run. Frost on an exposed pipe, a bulge in the line or a strange smell from a drain can also point to ice. If the water is out everywhere, the freeze may be at the meter or the main line.

A frozen pipe is not yet a burst pipe. Ice blocks flow, and the real damage comes when the ice expands and splits the pipe or a fitting. The split often shows only after the ice melts and water starts moving again.

  • Little or no water from one fixture.
  • Frost or ice on a visible pipe.
  • A bulge or crack in the pipe.
  • Bad smells from a drain.
  • Strange noises when water is turned on.

How to thaw a pipe safely

Do not use a torch, a propane heater or any open flame. Fire near framing and insulation is a bigger danger than the freeze. If you cannot reach the frozen section, or a pipe is already cracked, stop and call.

  1. Open the affected faucet so water has a place to go as the ice melts.
  2. Find the frozen section, often along an exterior wall or in an unheated space.
  3. Warm it slowly with a hair dryer, a heating pad or towels soaked in hot water.
  4. Keep heating until full flow returns, then check every joint for drips.

When to shut off the main

If you see water, a split or a bulge, close the main valve right away. Then open a faucet at the lowest point of the house to drain the line. Switch off power to the water heater if the tank may have lost water, since a heating element running dry can burn out.

Know where your main shutoff is before winter. It is usually near the meter or where the line enters the house. A homeowner who can find it in the dark saves a lot of damage. The burst pipe repair page covers what happens next if a pipe has already split.

Where Huntsville pipes freeze first

In houses around Huntsville, the weak spots are outdoor spigots, supply lines in exterior walls, pipes in attics and anything in an open crawl space. Rentals and older homes near the courthouse square often have lines that were never insulated. Mobile and manufactured homes can have exposed lines under the floor.

The same weak spots exist in New Waverly and Riverside, where rural properties often have longer exposed runs from the well or meter to the house.

Prevent the next freeze

On a freeze night, let a faucet drip, open cabinet doors under sinks on exterior walls and keep the thermostat steady. Disconnect hoses and cover outdoor spigots. Foam sleeves on exposed pipes cost little and help a lot.

If you leave town, keep the heat on and ask someone to check the house. A frozen pipe that thaws while no one is home can run for days. Water leaks that go unnoticed are what leak detection is for, and a spinning meter with every fixture off is a warning sign worth acting on.

When to call a plumber

Call if you cannot find the frozen section, if a pipe is cracked, if water appears anywhere it should not be or if the thawed pipe leaks. A plumber can find the break, replace the damaged section and check the rest of the line.

Insurance may cover sudden water damage from a burst pipe. Photograph the damage before you clean up and keep the receipts.

A local note on Huntsville water

The City of Huntsville's 2025 water quality report lists chloramines as the disinfectant, averaging 3.09 mg/L across the year. Chloraminated water is not a freeze risk, but it does wear rubber parts, which is why shutoff valves and washers that have not been touched in years can fail when you finally turn them. Exercise your main valve once a year so you know it works before an emergency.

Replacement sections of a split pipe are installed to the International Plumbing Code (IPC), which the City of Huntsville lists at its 2024 edition.
