Water, soil and why older lines fail

Walker County sits in East Texas forest country. The Texas State Historical Association describes the soils as sands and clays, and the county averages roughly 46 inches of rain a year. Wet and dry cycles move clay, and pine and oak roots search out leaking joints. Recurring clogs near the street often turn out to be a line problem and not a grease problem, which is the case for sewer line repair.

If you are on city water, the City of Huntsville's 2025 report lists chloramine as the disinfectant. Rubber flappers and seals wear faster in chloraminated water, so a running toilet is a common first sign.
