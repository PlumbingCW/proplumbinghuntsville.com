Emergency Water Heater Repair in Huntsville, TX

A cold shower is annoying, but a water heater leaking onto the floor is an emergency. Our 24/7 line takes both kinds of calls in Huntsville, and the first move is the same. If water is pooling, shut the cold supply valve above the tank. If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 before you call us.

Water heater repair means finding which part failed and replacing only that part. Most tank units have a short list of common failures, including a heating element, a thermostat, a gas control valve or a pilot assembly. The visit ends with a clear answer on whether repair or replacement makes more sense.

No hot water: the common causes

A water heater that makes no hot water at all usually has lost its heat source. On an electric unit that is often a burned lower element, a tripped high limit switch or a failed thermostat. On a gas unit it is typically a pilot that will not stay lit, a bad thermocouple or a gas valve that is not opening.

Lukewarm water points elsewhere. A broken dip tube lets cold water mix in at the top of the tank, and sediment on the bottom insulates the burner or covers the lower element. Each problem has a different fix, which is why a quick diagnosis beats guessing at parts.

  • No hot water at all: check the breaker or pilot, then call.
  • Hot water that runs out in minutes: element or dip tube.
  • Rumbling or popping from the tank: sediment buildup.
  • Water that smells of rotten eggs: reaction at the anode rod.

Leaking water heater: what to do in the first ten minutes

Where the water comes from matters. A drip from the temperature and pressure relief valve or its discharge pipe often means a failing valve or excess pressure and may be fixable. Water seeping from the tank body or the seam at the bottom means the steel has corroded through, and no repair will fix that.

Shut the cold water supply valve on the pipe above the heater, turn the thermostat down or the breaker off, and leave the gas valve alone unless you can reach it safely. If the tank sits in a garage or closet, move stored items away from the water. Then call, because a tank that leaks slowly can release a full load of water with little warning.

If the tank is done, our water heater replacement crew can size and set a new one.

How a repair visit works

  1. The plumber confirms fuel type, tank size and age from the data label, and asks what the heater was doing before it stopped.
  2. Power or gas is tested. On electric units that means checking the elements and thermostats with a meter. On gas units it means the pilot, thermocouple and gas control.
  3. Safety parts are inspected, including the relief valve, the discharge pipe and the venting on a gas unit.
  4. You get the repair price and the alternative in plain terms before any part is installed.

Chloramine, local water and the parts that wear first

The City of Huntsville's 2025 water quality report lists chloramines as its disinfectant, with a 2025 average of 3.09 mg/L. Many plumbers see rubber and plastic parts, such as seals and fill valves, wear faster in chloraminated water than in water treated with free chlorine. It is a pattern to watch, not a guarantee for any one house.

The same report shows the city's supply comes from groundwater wells plus surface water purchased from the TRA Huntsville Regional system. The report does not give a hardness figure, so test your tap or ask the water utility for its numbers. Hard water leaves scale on elements and the tank floor, and a yearly flush is cheap insurance on any tank heater.
