How To Repair a Leaky Sink Faucet in Huntsville, TX

A dripping faucet wastes water and usually means a worn part, not a broken faucet. Most drips come from a washer, an O-ring or a cartridge, and the repair costs little if you catch it early.

The key is to identify your faucet type, turn the water off and replace the worn part.

Written by the Huntsville Pro Plumbing team · Published November 13, 2026.

Find your faucet type

There are four common types. A compression faucet has two handles and uses rubber washers. A cartridge faucet has one or two handles and a replaceable cartridge. A ball faucet has a single handle on a ball. A ceramic disc faucet has a single handle and ceramic plates. The fix depends on the type.

Look at the brand name on the faucet or under the sink, and match the part before you buy it.

TypeCommon leakPart to replace
CompressionDrip from the spoutWasher and seat.
CartridgeDrip or leak at the handleCartridge and O-rings.
BallLeak at the baseSprings, seals and cam.
Ceramic discDrip from the spoutCylinder or seals.

Step by step

Take the old part to the store to match it. Wrap threads with plumber's tape where the instructions call for it.

  1. Close the shutoff valves under the sink and open the faucet to release pressure.
  2. Plug the drain so small parts cannot fall in.
  3. Remove the handle, decorative cap and retaining nut to reach the cartridge, washer or seal.
  4. Replace the worn part, reassemble, open the shutoffs slowly and check for drips.

Why faucets leak in the first place

Rubber wears. Chloramine in city water can speed it. The City of Huntsville's report lists chloramine as the disinfectant, so rubber washers and O-rings may need replacing more often than in other places. Scale from hard water can also scratch seats and cartridges. The report gives no hardness figure, so test your tap.

A faucet that leaks at the base or under the sink may have a failing supply line, which is a different repair.

Tools and parts to gather

Photograph each step as you disassemble the faucet so you can put the parts back in the right order. Keep the screws in the bowl, since a screw down the drain is a slow way to end a simple job.

  • Adjustable wrench and screwdrivers.
  • Allen keys for set screws.
  • Plumber's tape and a flashlight.
  • Replacement cartridge, washer or O-ring set.
  • A towel and a small bowl for screws.

Repair or replace?

A repair fits when the faucet is sound and parts are available. Replacement fits when the faucet is corroded, parts are discontinued or the repair would cost close to a new one. Newer faucets also use less water. Our faucet repair page covers what a plumber does, and plumbing installation covers fixture swaps.

If water leaks under the sink or the shutoffs will not close, call a plumber so the fix does not turn into a flood.

When it is not just a drip

A faucet that will not shut off, a handle that sprays or a shutoff valve that does not stop the flow needs prompt attention. Close the main shutoff and call. We serve Huntsville, New Waverly and Riverside around the clock.

Signs the shutoff valves are the real problem

Old shutoff valves under a sink often do not close fully or leak when turned. If water keeps flowing after you close them, do not force them. Use the main shutoff instead, and ask a plumber to replace the valves while the faucet is apart.

New valves cost little, and they turn a future leak into a thirty second fix.

A local note on parts and supply

The City of Huntsville reports chloramine at an average of 3.09 mg/L with a range of 1.12 to 3.92 mg/L. When you replace a faucet part, choose chloramine-resistant rubber such as EPDM where available, since standard rubber can wear faster in chloraminated water.

Faucet and shutoff valve connections are covered by the International Plumbing Code (IPC), which the City of Huntsville lists as its plumbing code at the 2024 edition.

Source type: City of Huntsville 2025 Water Quality Report
