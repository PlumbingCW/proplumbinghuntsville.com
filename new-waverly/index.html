Water, pipes and soil around New Waverly

Homes outside city limits often use a private well or a small water system and not the City of Huntsville supply. If you are on a well, water quality varies by depth and location, and a lab test is the right first step before you buy treatment equipment. Our water softener and filtration page explains what to test.

The Texas State Historical Association describes Walker County soils as sands and clays. Clay swells when wet and shrinks when dry, and that movement can stress buried lines at joints. A recurring backup on an older property may point to a shifted or root-invaded line rather than a simple clog.
