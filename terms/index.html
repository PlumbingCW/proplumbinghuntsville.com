Terms of Use

These terms cover your use of this website and our phone dispatch line.

About this site

The information on this site is general guidance about plumbing and is not a substitute for an on-site assessment. Prices depend on the job and are agreed with the plumber before work begins.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. By calling, you agree to that recording.

Service and scheduling

Appointments are subject to plumber availability, and same-day service is offered when available. Emergency dispatch is available around the clock, but arrival times depend on location and demand and are not guaranteed.

For gas leaks, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 before calling us.

Links and changes

This site may link to government and industry sites such as the City of Huntsville and the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners. We do not control those sites.

We may update these terms, and the current version is always on this page.
