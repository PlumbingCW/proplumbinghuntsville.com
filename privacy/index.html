Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information we handle when you call Huntsville Pro Plumbing or visit this website.

Calls to our line

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. When you call, you may give a name, a street address and a description of your plumbing problem. We use that information to dispatch a plumber and to follow up on the job.

This site has no forms, so it does not collect details through the page itself. The only way to reach us from the site is by phone or by emailing the address in the footer.

Website information

Like most sites, the web server may log basic technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit. We use this to keep the site running and to understand which pages are useful.

We do not sell the details you give us over the phone.

Your choices

If you would like a call recording or your details removed, email contact@proplumbinghuntsville.com with your phone number and the date of the call, and we will respond. We may keep records that the law requires us to keep.

This policy may be updated from time to time, and the current version is always on this page.
