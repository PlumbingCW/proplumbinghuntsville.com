Emergency Hydro Jetting in Huntsville, TX

When a drain backs up and a cable only punches a hole through the blockage, hydro jetting is the next step. Our 24/7 line takes jetting calls in Huntsville for backups that cannot wait, and while you wait you should stop using water so nothing more is sent into the line.

Hydro jetting is a drain cleaning method that sends high pressure water through a hose and nozzle to scour the inside of a pipe. It removes grease, scale and roots that a cable leaves behind, and it is used on main lines, kitchen lines and commercial drains.

When a backup calls for jetting

A cable machine cuts a channel through a stoppage. If the pipe wall remains coated, the channel narrows again within weeks. Jetting washes the wall itself, which is why it suits repeat clogs, heavy grease and root-filled lines.

Call right away when a cable has stalled, when a line has been cleared more than once in a few months or when a restaurant or rental needs a line open before service. For one-time blockages, drain cleaning with a cable is usually enough.

  • A cable got stuck partway down a line.
  • Drain clogs again within weeks of cleaning.
  • Grease buildup in a kitchen or commercial line.
  • Root hairs in a camera recording that a cable did not remove.

How jetting works

  1. The plumber runs a camera first to see the pipe and avoid jetting a broken line.
  2. A hose with a forward and backward spray nozzle goes into the cleanout under controlled pressure.
  3. Water pulls the nozzle forward while the spray scours the pipe wall and pushes debris toward the main.
  4. A second camera pass confirms the wall is clean and shows any damage the jetting uncovered.

Jetting is not right for every pipe

A cracked, collapsed or heavily corroded pipe can be damaged by high pressure water. That is why the camera comes first. If footage shows a failing pipe, the plumber will recommend repair under sewer line repair rather than jetting.

Older clay and cast iron lines need lower pressure and a careful plumber, and some drain materials should not be jetted at all. Ask which pressure and nozzle will be used on your line.

Jetting for restaurants and rentals

A commercial kitchen sends fat down the line all day, and a cable clears it only briefly. Jetting is how many restaurants keep floor drains and grease lines flowing between pumping visits. Landlords use it for repeat blockages that tenants report every few months.

Before and after: what to expect

Before jetting, a plumber usually runs a camera to see the pipe, since jetting is not right for every line. Cracked or collapsed pipe can be damaged by high pressure. After jetting, a second camera pass shows the pipe wall and confirms the cleaning worked.

Expect some noise and water flow during the work. The plumber sets up at a cleanout, so the work is mostly outside the house.
