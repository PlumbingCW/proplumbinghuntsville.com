  • Switch off power to wet equipment, including fryers and dishwashers.
  • Move stock and paper records off the floor.
  • Photograph the damage for your insurer.

What commercial plumbing covers

Commercial plumbing involves larger pipes, higher demand and often more regulation than residential work. A restaurant needs grease management and floor drains that keep up with dishwashing. A shop needs reliable restrooms. An apartment building needs a water heater that serves many units.

Many problems are the same as at a home, such as a clogged drain, which our drain cleaning page covers, or a failed heater, which is on the water heater repair page. The difference is scale, schedule and the cost of downtime.

Grease, drains and recurring backups

Restaurants and food service send fat, oil and food scraps into drains daily. A cable clears a stoppage, but grease on the pipe wall rebuilds. Hydro jetting cleans the wall and is a routine maintenance step for many kitchens.

Grease traps and interceptors have their own pumping schedules set by local rules. Ask the City of Huntsville which requirements apply to your business, since this page cannot state the local rule for you.

Permits and licensed work in Huntsville

The City of Huntsville states that commercial permits are required for all new plumbing systems and for water or waste piping replacements. It also states that plumbing systems must be handled by individuals licensed by the state of Texas. A plumber who works in your building should be able to show a license number.

Ask for the Responsible Master Plumber's license number and check it in the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners license search before work begins. The number should appear on written estimates and invoices.
