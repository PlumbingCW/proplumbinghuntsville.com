Emergency Slab Leak Repair in Huntsville, TX

A slab leak is a break in a water line that runs beneath your concrete foundation, and it can run for weeks before anyone notices. If you hear running water with everything off, feel a warm spot on the floor or see your meter moving, call our 24/7 line and, while you wait, shut the main valve to stop the loss.

Slab leak repair means locating the exact failure, then choosing between a spot repair through the slab, rerouting the line through the attic or walls, or repiping. The plumber explains the options and what each involves before any concrete is cut.

Signs of a slab leak you should not ignore

A slab leak rarely announces itself with a flood. The first clue is usually the water bill, followed by a meter that moves with every fixture off. Hot water slab lines also leave warm patches on tile or hardwood, and a sound like trickling water under the floor at night is a common sign.

Cracks in tile, damp carpet near baseboards, a drop in water pressure and mold smell near the foundation edge are other flags. Because water under a slab can undermine the soil, treat these symptoms as urgent rather than waiting for the next billing cycle.

  • Meter moves with all fixtures off.
  • Warm or hot spot on the floor.
  • Sound of running water under the floor.
  • Unexplained jump in the water bill.
  • Damp carpet, cracked tile or musty smell along walls.

What to do while you suspect a slab leak

  • Close the main valve when no one is home.
  • Do not run the water heater if the leak is on the hot line.
  • Keep rugs and furniture off warm floor patches.
  • Note where the floor feels warm or damp.
  • Call the dispatcher before moving anything heavy.

Why slab leaks happen in this part of Texas

The Texas State Historical Association describes Walker County's forest soils as sands and clays. Clay-rich soil swells when wet and shrinks when dry, and that movement can stress pipes cast in or running under a slab. Pinhole leaks from corrosion, abrasion where a line rubs concrete and a failed joint are the other common causes.

The City of Huntsville's permit page lists a pier, foundation and ground plumbing inspection as the first plumbing inspection on new work, which tells you the ground plumbing stage is a recognized step in local construction. Older homes may have copper or galvanized lines in that zone, and a plumber can tell you what yours has.

How a slab leak is located and fixed

  1. The plumber isolates hot and cold lines and uses a pressure test to find which one is losing water.
  2. Acoustic equipment and a pipe locator trace the line under the floor to the exact point of the leak.
  3. You are shown three options: a spot repair by opening the slab above the leak, rerouting the line overhead, or repiping the affected zone.
  4. After repair the line is pressure tested again and the plumber confirms the meter is still.

A single pinhole in a newer line is a good candidate for a spot repair. Several failures in the same run point to rerouting or repiping.
