Emergency Sewer Line Cleaning in Huntsville, TX

When the main sewer line backs up, every drain in the house is affected, and the lowest fixtures overflow first. Stop using water and call our 24/7 line in Huntsville. A plumber with a main-line machine can usually clear the stoppage the same day and often the same night.

Sewer line cleaning clears the large pipe that carries all of a home's wastewater out to the city main or septic tank. It uses a heavy cable machine or water jet to remove roots, grease and debris, then a flow test and often a camera pass to confirm the pipe is open.

Main line backup: act before it reaches the floor

A main line stoppage sends wastewater to the lowest opening in the house, which is usually a shower, tub or floor drain on the ground floor. A toilet on the main level can overflow with the next flush. Do not run laundry or dishwashers and do not flush.

If you can, open the exterior cleanout cap slowly. Standing water in the cleanout confirms the blockage is downstream of the house. Do not open it if you see sewage rising. The plumber will use the cleanout to run the cable.

Cable, jet or camera: how the clean-out is done

For roots and thick debris, a motorized cable with a cutting head works through the blockage. Grease and fine scale call for high pressure water, which our hydro jetting page covers. A camera pass afterward shows whether the pipe is simply dirty or actually damaged.

The camera matters. Clearing a line without looking at it is like treating a cough without checking the lungs. If the footage shows a crack or offset, the next step is a repair quote and not another cleaning, which is where sewer line repair takes over.

Preventing the next main line backup

Do not flush wipes, even those labeled flushable, and keep grease and coffee grounds out of kitchen drains. If you have large trees near the line, a periodic cleaning and camera check is cheaper than an emergency call. Know where your cleanout is so a plumber can reach the line quickly.

For Huntsville homes on septic, avoid chemical drain cleaners and have the tank pumped on the schedule your tank size calls for.

After-hours service and what to expect on site

  1. The dispatcher asks which fixtures are affected, whether you have a cleanout and whether sewage is on the floor.
  2. The plumber arrives with a main-line machine, locates the cleanout and runs the cable to the blockage.
  3. Water is run through the line and the plumber confirms flow, then recommends a camera pass if one was not done.
  4. You get the price before work starts and a written explanation of what was found.

Ask your insurer whether you carry a sewer backup endorsement, since cleaning itself is usually not covered but resulting damage sometimes is. Call to get a price on your line.

Main sewer line questions answered

Yes. The 24/7 line takes main line backup calls day and night.

Many cleanings take one to two hours once the plumber has reached the cleanout, with a camera pass adding some time.

Roots, grease buildup, a sagging pipe or a cracked joint cause repeat stoppages. A camera look shows which.

Chemicals rarely clear a main line and can harm pipes and septic tanks. A cable or jetter is the reliable fix.
