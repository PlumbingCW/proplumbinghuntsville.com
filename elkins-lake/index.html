Plumber in Elkins Lake, Huntsville, TX

Elkins Lake is a lake and golf community inside Huntsville, with wooded lots and streets such as East Lake Drive, Green Briar Drive and Rollingwood Drive. If something in your plumbing is wrong, call us and describe what is happening.

This page covers what is documented about the community, how Huntsville's plumbing rules apply to it and which services fit common household problems.

About the Elkins Lake community

The community's own website says Elkins Lake is fully incorporated into the City of Huntsville, and that the city provides fire, police, garbage and utility services. Listings place it in ZIP code 77340. A real estate information sheet describes roughly 1,000 rolling, wooded acres, with development beginning in the early 1970s. That sheet is undated, so treat its figures as approximate.

The same sheet counts about 2,200 platted home sites and around 1,200 homes built, set around a 102-acre main lake, two smaller lakes and a 27-hole golf course. The Elkins Lake Recreation Corporation, owned by its members and run by an elected board, maintains the recreation facilities and common areas. A separately elected Architectural Control Committee also exists, so check its rules before planning any exterior work.

Plumbing in a community built in phases

Homes here went up over several decades, and one Section 2 listing shows a 1992 build year. Sections platted at different times can have different fixtures and pipe materials, so we do not guess a pipe type from a street name. A plumber should look at your own house before saying what it has.

Many lots sit on rolling, wooded ground. That is general terrain and not a finding about any one house, but it is a good reason to ask two questions. Where does the main sewer line leave the house, and how are tree roots near it managed? A camera look answers both with a picture.

  • Slow or repeating drains in kitchens and bathrooms.
  • Water heater leaking, noisy or out of hot water.
  • Dripping faucets, running toilets and failed shutoff valves.
  • A damp spot, a high water bill or a meter that moves with everything off.
  • Sewer backup or gurgling at the lowest fixture in the house.

City rules that apply inside Elkins Lake

Because Elkins Lake sits inside Huntsville, City permit rules apply here. The City requires permits for new plumbing systems and for replacing water or waste piping, and inspections follow set stages. It lists the International Plumbing Code (IPC) 2024 among the codes it uses.

Texas generally requires plumbing work to be done by a licensed plumber, with limited exceptions such as a homeowner working on their own home. You can check any plumber's license in the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners public search. Ask for the license number before work starts.

Before you call about a leak

  • Find the main shutoff valve and close it if water is spreading.
  • Switch off power to any wet room or wet appliance.
  • Note where the problem started and when you first saw it.
  • Take photos for your records.
  • Have your street address ready.

Elkins Lake plumbing questions

The community's website says it is fully incorporated into the City of Huntsville. Confirm your own address with the City if the answer affects a permit or a utility account.

The community's website says the City of Huntsville provides utilities, among other services. Your utility bill shows who serves your address, and the City can answer account questions.

City of Huntsville permit rules apply. The City requires permits for new plumbing systems and for replacing water or waste piping, so ask the City permit office whether your job needs one.

As described by the community, its role is the recreation facilities and common areas. Plumbing inside a private home is normally the owner's responsibility.

A plumber can inspect exposed pipe at the water heater and under sinks, check the meter and run a camera down the sewer line. The year a house was built does not settle the question.
