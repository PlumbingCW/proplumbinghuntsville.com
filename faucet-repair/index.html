Emergency Faucet Repair in Huntsville, TX

A faucet or valve that will not shut off is an emergency when it is flooding a sink cabinet or a wall. Close the shutoff valve under the sink or the main valve and call our 24/7 line in Huntsville. If the valve itself will not turn, the plumber replaces it on the visit.

Faucet repair covers dripping spouts, worn cartridges, leaking handles, failed shutoff valves and supply lines under sinks. Most repairs replace a cartridge, washer or valve seat instead of the whole faucet, and the plumber tells you when replacing it makes more sense.

Valve will not shut off or water is spraying

A stuck angle stop under a sink, a burst supply line or a failed shower valve can release water quickly. Close the main valve at the house if the local shutoff will not turn. Do not force a corroded valve. The handle can snap and make the leak worse.

After-hours calls for a leaking valve or supply line are common, and the fix is usually same visit. The plumber replaces the stop or line and tests it. If the shutoff behind a toilet is stuck, toilet repair covers that as well.

What to do before a faucet repair

  • Close the shutoff valves under the sink.
  • If they do not close, shut the main valve.
  • Clear the cabinet so the plumber can work.
  • Do not force a stuck handle, since it can snap the stem.

Why faucets drip and what fixes them

Every drip has a source. Compression faucets drip when the washer or seat wears. Cartridge and ball faucets drip when the cartridge or seals wear. A slow drip wastes a surprising amount of water over a month, and the sound is often worse than the bill.

Mineral scale can also hold a valve open. The Huntsville city water report gives no hardness figure, so testing your tap helps decide whether a filter or softener makes sense. See our water softener and filtration page for that.

  • Drip from the spout: worn cartridge or washer.
  • Leak at the base of the handle: worn O-ring.
  • Low flow: clogged aerator or valve.
  • Wet cabinet under the sink: supply line or trap leak.

Under-sink leaks and shower valves

Wet cabinets are often a supply line, a trap connection or a garbage disposal seal, not the faucet. A plumber traces the water to its source. Shower valve leaks that show as staining on an adjacent wall need a quicker response because they run inside the wall.

If water is running behind a tub wall or you see a ceiling stain below a bathroom, call right away and see leak detection.

How a faucet visit works

  1. The plumber shuts the water at the local valve and identifies the faucet brand and cartridge type.
  2. The faulty part is removed and compared with the replacement.
  3. The new part is installed, the aerator is cleaned and the faucet is tested on both hot and cold.
  4. You see the work and the plumber checks the supply lines and shutoffs while the cabinet is open.
